# The Rabjung 60-Year Cycle

**Rabjung** (Tib. `rab byung` རབ་བྱུང་; Ch. ràojiǒng 绕迥) is the Tibetan
60-year cycle that names each year by a combination of one of the **five
elements** and one of the **twelve animals**. The first cycle is traditionally
counted from **1027 CE** - the year the Kālacakra Tantra (`dus kyi 'khor lo`
དུས་ཀྱི་འཁོར་ལོ་) chronology is said to have been introduced to Tibet -
beginning with the **female fire-hare** year (`me mo yos` མེ་མོ་ཡོས་).

## Key Terms

| Term | Wylie | Tibetan | Chinese (pinyin / hanzi) | Notes |
|------|-------|---------|--------------------------|-------|
| 60-year cycle | `rab byung` | རབ་བྱུང་ | ràojiǒng 绕迥 | Named after its first year. |
| element | `'byung ba` / `khams` | འབྱུང་བ་ / ཁམས་ | wǔxíng 五行 | Wood, fire, earth, iron, water. |
| animal year | `lo` + animal | ལོ་ | shēngxiào 生肖 | 12 animals as in the Chinese zodiac. |
| fire-hare year | `me yos lo` | མེ་ཡོས་ལོ་ | huǒtù nián 火兔年 | First year of the cycle (1027). |

## Structure

- Each element governs **two consecutive years** (male `pho` ཕོ་ and female
  `mo` མོ་), and the twelve animals cycle once per element pairing, producing
  5 × 12 = 60 distinct year names.
- The animals match the Chinese zodiac list - rat (`byi ba` བྱི་བ་), ox
  (`glang` གླང་), tiger (`stag` སྟག་), hare (`yos` ཡོས་), dragon (`brug`
  འབྲུག་), snake (`sbrul` སྦྲུལ་), horse (`rta` རྟ་), sheep (`lug` ལུག་),
  monkey (`spre'u` སྤྲེའུ་), bird (`bya` བྱ་), dog (`khyi` ཁྱི་), pig
  (`phag` ཕག་) - but the element-animal alignment is offset from the Chinese
  sexagenary cycle; see [[tibetan-chinese-relation]].
- A year is cited as, e.g., "the male wood-dragon year of the Nth rabjung."

## Notes on Traditions

Sources differ on the count's origin: the Indian-derived reckoning begins in
1027 CE (fire-hare), while a Chinese-style reckoning counts from a male
wood-rat year (1024 CE). Both are presented in the sources as traditional
conventions.
