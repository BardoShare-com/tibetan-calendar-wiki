# Tibetan Calendar Wiki

A research-focused reference for the Tibetan calendar (Tib. `bod kyi rtsis`
བོད་ཀྱི་རྩིས་; Ch. zànglì 藏历), the lunisolar calendar of Tibetan Buddhism
derived from the Kālacakra (`dus kyi 'khor lo` དུས་ཀྱི་འཁོར་ལོ་; Ch. shílún
时轮) astronomical system. Tibetan terms are given in Wylie transliteration
and Tibetan Unicode script, with Chinese (pinyin and hanzi) equivalents where
they exist.

## Festivals and Holy Days

- [[tibetan-losar]] - Tibetan New Year (`lo gsar` ལོ་གསར་)
- [[tibetan-monlam-chenmo]] - Great Prayer Festival (`smon lam chen mo`
  སྨོན་ལམ་ཆེན་མོ་)
- [[tibetan-saga-dawa]] - Saga Dawa (`sa ga zla ba` ས་ག་ཟླ་བ) and Saga Dawa
  Düchen (`sa ga zla ba'i dus chen` ས་ག་ཟླ་བའི་དུས་ཆེན་)
- [[tibetan-chokhor-duchen]] - Chökhor Düchen (`chos 'khor dus chen`
  ཆོས་འཁོར་དུས་ཆེན་), First Turning of the Dharma Wheel
- [[tibetan-lhabab-duchen]] - Lhabab Düchen (`lha bab dus chen`
  ལྷ་བབས་དུས་ཆེན་), the Buddha's descent from Trāyastriṃśa heaven

## Recurring Holy Days of the Month

- [[tibetan-shakyamuni-day]] - Śākyamuni day (`shAkya thub pa`
  ཤཱཀྱ་ཐུབ་པ་), new moon / 30th
- [[tibetan-medicine-buddha-day]] - Medicine Buddha day (`sman bla`
  སྨན་བླ་), the 8th
- [[tibetan-guru-rinpoche-day]] - Guru Rinpoche day (`gu ru rin po che`
  གུ་རུ་རིན་པོ་ཆེ་), the 10th
- [[tibetan-dakini-day]] - Ḍākinī day (`mkha' 'gro ma'i dus`
  མཁའ་འགྲོ་མའི་དུས་), the 25th
